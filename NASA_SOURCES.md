# NASA educational resources used in this update

These are NASA conceptual educational resources, not live datasets or numerical inputs. They were reviewed and added during submission preparation on 15 November 2026. They have not been verified as entries in the challenge's separate Resources list. No NASA endorsement is claimed.

## Life Support Subsystems

https://www.nasa.gov/reference/jsc-life-support-subsystems/

Used in: Science Notebook and contextual debriefs for Missions 1–3. The learning text connects oxygen generation, water recovery and carbon dioxide removal to life-support engineering. The game explicitly notes that CO2 removal is not modeled separately. Power costs and production rates remain original teaching assumptions.

## How NASA Will Protect Astronauts From Space Radiation at the Moon

https://science.nasa.gov/science-research/heliophysics/how-nasa-will-protect-astronauts-from-space-radiation-at-the-moon/

Used in: Science Notebook and contextual debriefs for Missions 4–5. The educational explanation links shelter use and available shielding material to crew protection. Modeled shelter transit, energy costs and health penalties are invented and do not calculate radiation dose.

The six-slide demonstration also explains these resource-to-lesson connections. The original NASA page imagery and NASA marks are not copied into generated artwork. The game contains links and original paraphrases.
